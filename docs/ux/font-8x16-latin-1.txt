Egg Mono 8x16, the Latin-1 supplement and the euro sign: U+00A0-U+00FF and U+20AC, 97 glyphs
in codepoint order, the companion of font-8x16.txt (U+0020-U+007E). Original design by Claude
Fable for EggOS (D16, D185).
License: MIT OR Apache-2.0, like the rest of EggOS.
Format: a block per glyph, blocks separated by one empty line. A block is a header line "U+XXXX c"
(the codepoint in four upper-case hex digits and the character; U+00A0 is written NBSP and U+00AD
SHY), then 16 rows of 8 columns, "." for background and "#" for foreground, row 0 at the top.
Metrics are font-8x16.txt's: baseline row 12, capitals rows 2-12, x-height rows 5-12, descenders
rows 13-15, column 7 the letter gap (empty in every glyph). Accented capitals are the ASCII
capitals unchanged with the accent in rows 0-1; A-ring's ring takes rows 0-2 and its A starts at
row 3. Accented small letters are the ASCII small letters unchanged with the accent in rows 2-3
and row 4 empty (a-ring's ring takes rows 2-4); the i-forms drop the dot. The cedilla is rows
13-14, the descenders of y-acute, y-diaeresis and thorn rows 13-15. Accent shapes, one per family:
acute (4,0)(3,1) as (column, row within the accent) counted from the left, grave (2,0)(3,1),
circumflex (3,0)(2,1)(4,1), diaeresis columns 2 and 5 in both rows (columns 2 and 4 on I and i),
tilde "..##.#.." over ".#.##...", ring a 4-wide loop on capitals and a 3-wide one on small
letters. The copyright and registered signs use all seven columns; the fractions set a 3x5
numerator at columns 0-2, rows 2-6, a slash from (6,4) to (2,8) and a 3x5 denominator at columns
4-6, rows 8-12. See docs/ux/keyboard-v0.3.md section 8.
