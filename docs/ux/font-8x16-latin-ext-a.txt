Egg Mono 8x16, the Latin Extended-A supplement: U+0100-U+017F, 128 glyphs in codepoint order
(Y-diaeresis, U+0178, the capital Latin-1 lacks, is among them), the companion of font-8x16.txt
(U+0020-U+007E) and font-8x16-latin-1.txt (U+00A0-U+00FF, U+20AC).
Original design by Claude Fable for EggOS (D16, D220; docs/ux/keyboard-v0.3.md section 9.8).
License: MIT OR Apache-2.0, like the rest of EggOS.
Format: font-8x16-latin-1.txt's: a block per glyph, blocks separated by one empty line. A block is
a header line "U+XXXX c" (the codepoint in four upper-case hex digits and the character), then 16
rows of 8 columns, "." for background and "#" for foreground, row 0 at the top.
Metrics are font-8x16.txt's: baseline row 12, capitals rows 2-12, x-height rows 5-12, descenders
rows 13-15, column 7 the letter gap (empty in every glyph). Every letter keeps its ASCII body
pixel for pixel; the accent sits in the rows the font reserves: rows 0-1 on capitals and on the
small letters with an ascender (h, l; d's caron sits over its ascender at columns 4-6), rows 2-3
on the other small letters with row 4 empty, and the dotless i-forms drop the dot. Marks below
take rows 13-14. Shapes, as (column, row) within the accent counted from the left: acute (4,0)(3,1),
grave (2,0)(3,1), circumflex (3,0)(2,1)(4,1), caron the circumflex upside down (2,0)(4,0)(3,1),
breve ".#...#.." over "..###...", tilde "..##.#.." over ".#.##...", diaeresis columns 2 and 5 in
both rows, macron one row "..####.." at row 0 (capitals) or row 2 (small letters) with the row
under it empty, dot above one row at row 0 on capitals and the dot of i (rows 2-3) on small
letters, double acute "...#.#.." over "..#.#...", ring the Latin-1 rings (U-ring's U starts at
row 3, like A-ring's A), cedilla the 2-row hook of Latin-1 placed under the letter's stem or
centre, ogonek a 2-row hook ".....#.." over ".....##." hanging from the letter's right (centred
under narrow letters), the comma above of g-cedilla the cedilla's shape at rows 2-3. Strokes:
D-stroke is Latin-1's Eth; d-stroke bars its ascender at row 3; H-stroke bars row 4 across all
seven columns; h-stroke bars columns 0-2 at row 3; L-stroke adds (2,6) and (0,8) to L's stem;
l-stroke adds (4,7) and (2,9); T-stroke bars columns 2-4 at row 6; t-stroke columns 1-3 at
row 9. The caron on d, L, l and t is the apostrophe form (a 2-pixel vertical stroke at rows
2-3 right of the letter's top). Ligatures share one spine at column 3 like ae: OE is a 4-wide
O (columns 0-3, rounded) and a 4-wide E (columns 3-6); oe likewise at x-height; IJ sets I in
columns 0-2 and J in columns 4-6 with J's hook at row 11-12; ij the same with both dots. Kra is a
k without its ascender; eng continues the right stem of N or n three rows down to a 4-wide foot
at row 15; long s is an f without its bar. See docs/ux/keyboard-v0.3.md section 9.8.
