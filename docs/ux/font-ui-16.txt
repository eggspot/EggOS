Egg Sans 16, the EggOS UI font: a proportional 1-bit bitmap face for every label, button,
list row and heading. Egg Mono 8x16 (font-8x16.txt) stays the terminal's and code's font.
Original design by Claude Fable for EggOS v0.3.0 (D16).
License: MIT OR Apache-2.0, like the rest of EggOS.
Format: free-text header, then one block per glyph, blocks separated by one empty line. A block
is a header line "U+XXXX c W" (codepoint, the character or SPACE / NBSP / SHY, the advance width
W in pixels), then 16 rows of exactly W characters, "." background and "#" ink, row 0 at the top.
Coverage: U+0020-U+007E, U+00A0-U+00FF and U+20AC, 192 glyphs in codepoint order.
Metrics (rows count from 0 at the top): cell height 16, line pitch 20 in body text; baseline is
row 12 (the last ink row of capitals and x-height letters), shared with Egg Mono; cap height
10 rows (3-12); x-height 7 rows (6-12); ascenders of b d f h k l and the bar of t start at row 2
(11 rows); descenders of g j p q y use rows 13-15 (3 rows); the dots of i and j and the accents
of small letters use rows 3-4 (the ring of a-ring rows 2-4), row 5 stays empty; the accents of
capitals use rows 0-1, row 2 stays empty; the ring of A-ring uses rows 0-2 and that A is drawn
one row shorter (rows 4-12); the cedilla uses rows 13-15.
Widths: the advance width includes the glyph's right-side spacing (one empty column for every
letter, digit and most signs; none for the underscore so underscores join), so text is set by
placing glyphs side by side with no extra gap. Space 4; the digits 0-9 are all 6 (tabular);
capitals mostly 6 (I 4, E F J L 5, M W 8); small letters mostly 6 (i l 2, f j t 4, k r 5,
m w 8); the widest glyphs (copyright, registered, ae, the fractions) are 10. Strokes are 1 px, no anti-aliasing, no
kerning, no second weight; 2x is the same bitmap with every pixel doubled. Dots of punctuation
are 2x2 like Egg Mono's; the dots of i, j and the diaeresis are 1x2. Zero carries the same
slash as Egg Mono's. Any codepoint outside the coverage is drawn as U+003F.
